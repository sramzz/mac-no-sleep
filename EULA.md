## 3. System Management and Hardware Disclaimer

Prevent Sleep modifies macOS power management settings (`SleepDisabled`) using Apple's supported system services (`/usr/bin/pmset`). 

**Important Hardware Notice:**
- When Prevent Sleep is set to **ON**, the operating system will not sleep automatically, even when the MacBook display or lid is closed.
- **Do not store your laptop in an enclosed bag, sleeve, or unventilated space while Prevent Sleep is active.** Running high-intensity processes with the lid closed inside an enclosed container may cause the computer to reach high thermal temperatures and drain the battery.
- Prevent Sleep includes an automated low-battery safeguard designed to restore normal sleep when the battery falls to or below 15% on battery power; however, you remain responsible for monitoring your hardware power state.
